## Kullanım koşulları

*2026-10-25*

### Dingin nedir

Kısa ölçekler ve telefonla yapılan testlerle çınlamanız için ön değerlendirme yapan, hangi hekime ne zaman gitmeniz gerektiğini söyleyen ve kanıta dayalı bir öz yönetim programı (ses terapisi, bilişsel-davranışçı egzersizler, uyku ve gevşeme) sunan bir uygulamadır.

### Ne değildir

Hekim veya odyolog muayenesinin yerine geçmez. Telefonla yapılan testler tahmindir, klinik odyogram değildir. Ön değerlendirme bir hekim tarafından doğrulanmalıdır.

### Acil durumlar

Ani işitme kaybı, şiddetli baş dönmesi, yüzde veya kol-bacakta güçsüzlük ya da uyuşma, kendinize zarar verme düşüncesi varsa 112'yi arayın veya en yakın acil servise gidin. Uygulamayı beklemeyin.

### Ses güvenliği

Sesleri rahat ve düşük bir düzeyde tutun. Bir ses rahatsızlık veya ağrı verirse durdurun.

### Sorumluluk

Sağlığınızla ilgili kararlar hekiminizle birlikte verilir. Uygulamayı kendi takdirinizle kullanırsınız.

## Privacy notice

*2026-10-25*

### Summary

Dingin works without an account. Everything you enter — answers, test results, programme progress — is stored only on this device. Nothing is sent to us or to any server, and there is no advertising or tracking.

### Data controller

[DATA CONTROLLER]. Contact: [CONTACT].

### What is processed

Health-related answers (tinnitus questionnaires, mood screening, history questions, hearing and sound test results), programme progress and your settings. The microphone is used only to measure room noise before the hearing test; no sound is recorded. The motion sensor is used only for the balance recording.

### Why

To produce your preliminary assessment, suggest which doctor to see, build and adapt your personal programme. The legal basis is your explicit consent, given on the first screen.

### Where it is kept

Only in the app's storage on your device. Several people can use one device; each person's data is kept separately.

### Sharing

Data leaves the device only when you share it yourself (doctor report PDF, progress card, data export). You choose the recipient.

### Deleting

Settings → People → delete a person removes that person's data. Settings → My data → delete removes everything. Uninstalling the app deletes all data.

### Your rights

Under KVKK Article 11 you may ask whether your data is processed, request correction or deletion and object to processing. Because the data is only on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app; for anything else contact us.

### Children

The app is intended for adults (18+).

## Terms of use

*2026-10-25*

### What Dingin is

An app that gives a preliminary assessment of tinnitus from short questionnaires and phone-based tests, tells you which doctor to see and when, and offers an evidence-based self-management programme (sound therapy, cognitive-behavioural exercises, sleep and relaxation).

### What it is not

It is not a substitute for an examination by a doctor or an audiologist. Phone-based tests are estimates, not a clinical audiogram. The preliminary assessment must be confirmed by a doctor.

### Emergencies

If you have sudden hearing loss, severe dizziness, weakness or numbness of the face or limbs, or thoughts of harming yourself, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for the app.

### Sound safety

Keep sounds at a comfortable, low level. Stop if a sound causes discomfort or pain.

### Responsibility

Decisions about your health are made together with your doctor. Use the app at your own discretion.
